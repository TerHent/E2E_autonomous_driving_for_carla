1. Local Scene & Context Builder: Ingests the privileged CARLA SceneState JSON (surrounding actors, traffic lights, lanes) together with the long-term module's current route window, formatting nearby vehicles' positions, speeds, and accelerations into a compact tactical context.  
​2. Tactical Tool Suite (Scene Queries): Exposes typed query tools that the planner can invoke to inspect immediate hazards—such as Time-to-Collision (TTC), whether an adjacent lane is free, and current traffic light states.  
​3. Skill & Maneuver Library: Supplies procedural, tool-conditioned guidance ("skills") for executing concrete short-horizon maneuvers like merging, overtaking, emergency braking, giving way, and obeying traffic signs.  
​4. Experience & Memory Manager: Stores and retrieves past driving experiences (scene descriptions paired with verified reasoning traces) via vector similarity to provide few-shot guidance, while tracking active driving intentions and user preferences.  
​5. LLM Tactical Decision Maker: Prompts the centrally deployed LLM (Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, Gemma4 31B, or Mistral Medium 3.5) with the local context, recalled memories, and active skills to output a structured JSON Decision containing a high-level meta-action (e.g., accelerate, idle, decelerate, lane change), parameters, and a human-readable rationale.  
​6. Safety Verifier (Veto Gate): Evaluates every proposed LLM meta-action against deterministic safety rules (e.g., TTC thresholds and red-light compliance) and vetoes unsafe decisions before they reach the vehicle controller.  
​7. Execution Bridge & Timing Manager: Hands verified meta-actions to CARLA's BehaviorAgent / LocalPlanner (or MPC) running at 20 Hz, decoupling low-frequency LLM reasoning from high-frequency closed-loop vehicle control.  
​8. Decision Logger & Reflection Loop: Writes every state, rationale, safety verdict, and executed action to DecisionLog (JSON lines) and runs post-episode reflection to diagnose failures, rectify unsafe reasoning, and feed revised experiences back into memory and prompts.
