1. Meta-Action Executor: Executes the verified Decision every tick on CARLA's BasicAgent / LocalPlanner with its built-in hazard braking disabled, either as discrete meta-actions (accelerate / decelerate / idle -> target speed clamped to the route window and speed limit) or through an IDM car-following model whose desired speed and time gap come from the Decision; changes lanes only when the Decision asks for it, then re-attaches the planner to the long-term route.
2. Decision Buffer & Staleness Handling: Holds the latest verified Decision with the timestamp of the scene it was based on and decides how much it still counts, either by hard expiry (validity window or significant scene change → safe fallback) or by soft decay (rule-based scores of all meta-actions recomputed every tick, plus a bonus for the LLM's choice that fades with its age).
3. Fast Reflex Path (20 Hz): Re-runs the safety verifier on the currently executed action at every tick against the current SceneState, so emergency braking and red-light stops never wait for the LLM.
4. Async LLM Scheduler: Calls the tactical decision maker in the background (periodically at ~1 Hz and on triggers such as a TTC drop, a new upcoming event or a decision about to expire) while the simulation keeps ticking.
5. Latency Injector & Probe: Delivers decisions after a configurable delay counted in simulation ticks (reproducible runs) and measures the real API latency of the served models.
6. Closed-Loop Runner: Main loop in a separate scripts/run_closed_loop.py, reusing the existing ScenarioRunner, that ticks CARLA at 20 Hz, calls the long-term and tactical modules on their own schedules and drives the car through the executor instead of the Traffic Manager autopilot.
7. DecisionLog Writer (from TIMUR.md component 8, the reflection loop stays with Timur): Writes every received decision (scene timestamp, meta-action, parameters, rationale, safety verdict, model, latency) and every executed tick (executed action, decision age, staleness strategy, reflex override, applied control) as JSON lines for the reflection loop and the evaluation.
8. Execution Metrics: Computes per-run execution metrics (decision age at execution, reflex overrides, minimum TTC, comfort: peak deceleration and jerk); route-level outcomes (collisions, red-light violations, route completion) come from the shared evaluation script and the long-term Health Monitor.
